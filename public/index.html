Questions

Plain answers, no jargon.

Does GST apply to residential and commercial rent the same way?+
No. A home let as a residence to an unregistered tenant is exempt. Commercial rent from a GST-registered landlord carries 18% GST. A home let to a GST-registered tenant is usually taxed under reverse charge — the tenant pays. What counts is actual use, not the building's label.
My tenant pays GST, not me. Is that right?+
It can be. Since 10 October 2024, when an unregistered landlord rents commercial property to a GST-registered tenant, the tenant pays GST under reverse charge. From 16 January 2025 composition-scheme tenants are excluded. You do not add that GST to your bill.
Why is less money in my bank than the invoice?+
Most often TDS. Companies and firms usually deduct 10% of rent above Rs. 50,000 a month (GST excluded). Smaller individual tenants deduct 2% once — in March or when the tenancy ends. TDS is credited to you only when it shows in your Form 26AS/AIS.
Can I choose how my invoice looks?+
Yes — Classic Ledger, Modern Minimal or Professional Letterhead. All three carry the same fields and numbers; only the look changes.
Does it make next month's invoice by itself?+
In this local version you click "Generate this month's invoices" and it prepares drafts from your saved agreements — never twice for the same month. A local scheduler runs only while the app is open. Fully unattended monthly runs need the hosted version (planned).
Will it email the PDF to my tenant?+
Not yet. It opens an email draft in your own mail app; you attach the downloaded PDFs and press send. The practice outbox only simulates delivery and says so.
How do calendar imports work?+
Download the .ics file and open it in Google Calendar, Outlook or your phone. Each task has reminders 5 days and 1 day before. It is a snapshot — it won't change if a date is extended.
Does KirayaKhata file my returns?+
No. It prepares records and tells you what to do on the GST or income-tax portal. Filing and payment happen on the official portals, by you or your CA.
Do I still need a CA?+
For ordinary cases KirayaKhata does the arithmetic and keeps records. Unusual situations — co-ownership, SEZ tenants, mixed use, non-resident landlords, and anything marked "needs specialist review" — need a professional. The tax rules here are also marked for CA verification.
Where is my information kept?+
On this device, in your browser's storage. It is not cloud storage and not encrypted by us; clearing browser data deletes it. Use Export to keep a backup. The local server keeps only anonymous demo counts.
Is it available in Hindi?+
Yes. Use the EN / हिं button at the top. Invoice PDFs are produced in English for now.
